# Accepted risks

This is the record SDD stage 5 asks for: HIGH, MEDIUM and LOW findings that were declined or deferred, each with the owner's decision. A CRITICAL finding can never be accepted here; it blocks the merge until it is fixed. An entry records a decision about a known finding. It does not say the risk is gone, and each entry names the point at which it is looked at again.

IDs: L1 to L5 and I1 to I3 are the owner's labels for the LOW and INFO items of the round-3 reviews of the M5b I1 work (`docs/specs/m5b-frontend.md`, Status). They are not the plan's increments I1 to I5: the "I2" below is the second review item, not increment I2. By the owner's decision, review items L1, L2, L4 and I1 were fixed during increment I1; the four items below were deferred.

Two columns need care. "Owner decision" is the owner's call (2026-11-07, given in chat). "Why it is acceptable for now" is the reviewers' and the plan's rationale, recorded as they gave it; it is not the owner's words. In "Revisit at", the point for L3 is the owner's (moved to I4); the points for the other three are proposals that the owner has not set. On 2026-11-07 (round 5) the owner adopted the rationale of each of the four entries L3, L5, I2 and I3 as written; that adoption does not set the proposed revisit points. The entries D-1 and D-2 record the dependency audit of the I4-prep change (a scan of `uv.lock` and `deploy/requirements-serve.txt`, 2026-11-10); D is for dependency, and the owner decided both on 2026-11-10 in chat, in the words given in each row. The entries R5-1 to R5-4 were recorded in round 5; on 2026-11-08 the owner delegated the decisions on R5-1 to R5-4, R5-6, R5-7 and UC-1 to the llm-council (council 10) and adopted its verdict, recorded in each row.

| I3 | INFO | Three edge cases in the Lighthouse runner's stop, none of which holds the fixture port. (a) Chrome can outlive a SIGKILL escalation: `chrome-launcher` starts Chrome in its own session, so the kill sent to the Lighthouse process group never reaches it, and the only cleanup is the SIGTERM listener of `chrome-launcher`, which the escalation skips.
